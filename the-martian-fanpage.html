About the Film

Stranded. Alone.
140 Million Miles from Home.

During a manned mission to Mars, astronaut Mark Watney is presumed dead after a fierce storm and left behind by his crew. But Watney has survived and finds himself stranded on the hostile, barren planet.

With only meager supplies, Watney must draw upon his ingenuity, wit, and spirit to subsist and find a way to signal to Earth that he is alive. Meanwhile, NASA and a team of international scientists work tirelessly to bring him home.

Directed by Ridley Scott and starring Matt Damon, the film is a triumphant celebration of human resilience, science, and the will to survive. It reminds us that when the universe conspires against you, the only option is to science the shit out of it.

Survival Chronicle

Mission Log

Sol 6
The Storm. Mission Abort.
A catastrophic dust storm forces the Ares 3 crew to abort the mission. Mark Watney is struck by debris and left for dead. The crew launches for Earth.
Sol 7
Watney Awakens
Mark regains consciousness alone on Mars. With no communications and dwindling supplies, he begins assessing his situation inside the HAB.
Sol 14
The Potato Farm Begins
Using Martian soil, crew waste, and sheer ingenuity, Mark begins growing potatoes inside the HAB — enough to extend his survival by years.
Sol 63
NASA Spots Watney from Orbit
Satellite imagery analyst Mindy Park notices movement near the Ares 3 HAB. NASA realizes Watney is alive and begins emergency planning.
Sol 100
Communication Restored via Pathfinder
Watney drives to the Pathfinder rover landing site and repurposes it to establish a rudimentary ASCII communication channel with Earth.
Sol 461
HAB Breach — Crop Catastrophe
An airlock failure destroys the potato crop. With resupply options delayed, Watney must further ration supplies and begin the long drive to Schiaparelli crater.
Sol 549
Rescue — Return to the Hermes
In a daring maneuver, Watney launches in a stripped-down MAV. Commander Lewis tethers him 70 meters above Mars. Against all odds, he makes it home.

Behind the Scenes

Film Facts

📚
Born as a Blog
Andy Weir originally self-published The Martian in 2011 as free chapters on his personal website before it became a bestselling novel.
🌱
Real Botany
NASA confirmed that the potato-growing science in the film is largely accurate. They even attempted to grow potatoes in Mars-like soil as an experiment.
🎬
Filmed in Jordan
The Martian surface scenes were filmed in Wadi Rum, Jordan — nicknamed "Valley of the Moon" — thanks to its strikingly red, alien landscape.
🏆
Golden Globe Winner
The film won Best Motion Picture — Musical or Comedy at the Golden Globes, a categorization that surprised many given its sci-fi survival premise.
🎵
The Disco Soundtrack
Commander Lewis's love of 70s disco music was a deliberate plot device — it became the only music Watney had access to on Mars, creating a beloved running gag.
🔭
NASA Partnership
NASA consulted extensively on the film and used its release as a platform to announce real discoveries about liquid water on Mars — timed perfectly for opening weekend.
🚀
Matt Damon's Commitment
Matt Damon reportedly lost 40+ pounds for the role to portray the gradual physical deterioration of someone surviving on limited calories for over a year.
📡
Production Speed
Ridley Scott completed principal photography in just 70 days — a remarkably fast pace for a major sci-fi production of this scale and visual complexity.
